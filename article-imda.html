IMDA launches consultation on data protection rules for messaging services

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

The Infocomm Media Development Authority has opened a public consultation on proposed guidelines for how messaging platforms should handle user data, including contact lists, message metadata, and backup storage.

The draft framework sets out minimum standards for consent, data retention periods, and breach notification timelines. IMDA said the rules would apply to over-the-top messaging services that have a significant user base in Singapore, regardless of where the companies are headquartered.

Under the proposals, providers must clearly disclose what information is collected when users sync phone contacts or enable cloud backups. They would also be required to offer options for users to download or delete stored data upon request.

Industry groups and consumer advocates have until next month to submit feedback. IMDA said it expects to publish final guidelines in the first quarter of next year, with a transition period for affected platforms to update their privacy policies and technical controls.
