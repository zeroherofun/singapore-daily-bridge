Public Wi-Fi speeds upgraded at community centres islandwide

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

The Infocomm Media Development Authority has completed a rollout of faster public Wi-Fi at more than 100 community centres across Singapore. The upgraded network offers improved download speeds and more stable connections in common areas such as function halls and activity rooms.

IMDA worked with operators to replace older access points and expand coverage to previously underserved corners of larger centres. Users connect through the existing Wireless@SG login portal, with session limits adjusted to accommodate longer stays during classes and events.

Five additional centres in newer housing estates will come online by the end of the month. IMDA said it will publish a map of upgraded locations on its website so residents can check availability before visiting.
