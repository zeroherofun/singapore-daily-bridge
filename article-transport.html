Transport Updated

SMRT restores full service on North-South Line after morning signalling fault

Train services on the North-South Line returned to normal shortly before noon after a signalling fault caused delays of up to 25 minutes during the morning peak.

SMRT said engineers identified an intermittent track circuit issue near Bishan station and deployed backup procedures while repairs were carried out. Free bus bridging was activated at affected stations.

The Land Transport Authority said it is reviewing the incident with the operator and will publish a summary of findings within two weeks.
