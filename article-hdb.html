Housing Updated

HDB launches 5,000 new flats in mature estates amid strong demand

By David Tan 4 min read

The Housing Board has opened applications for about 5,000 Build-To-Order flats across Ang Mo Kio, Toa Payoh, Bedok and Queenstown, reflecting continued demand for homes in established neighbourhoods.

First-timer families will receive enhanced grants at selected projects, while singles may apply for two-room flexi units at designated sites. Balloting results are expected within three weeks of the closing date.

HDB said construction timelines vary by project, with most estates scheduled for completion between 2028 and 2030. Buyers are encouraged to review location, transport links and nearby amenities before submitting applications.
