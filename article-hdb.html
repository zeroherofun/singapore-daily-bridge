Lift upgrading works completed at Ang Mo Kio block ahead of schedule

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

Lift modernisation works at a 14-storey block in Ang Mo Kio have been completed two weeks ahead of the original timeline, the town council announced on Tuesday. All four lifts now feature updated control panels, smoother door mechanisms, and improved accessibility features for elderly residents.

Contractors worked in phases so that at least one lift remained in service throughout the three-month project. Residents received advance notice of temporary closures through notice boards and the town council's messaging channels.

The town council said feedback from residents has been largely positive, with many noting quieter operation and shorter wait times during morning peak hours. Similar upgrading works at two neighbouring blocks are scheduled to begin next quarter.
