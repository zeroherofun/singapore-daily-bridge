Cookie Policy

A plain explanation of what City News Bulletin stores on your device, why, and how to change your mind.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves to your browser so it can remember something between visits or between pages — for example, whether you’ve already responded to a cookie banner. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way and are covered by this notice too.

2. The choice you’re given

When you first visit City News Bulletin, a banner offers three options: Accept all, which allows the full set of cookies described below; Reject all, which limits us to the strictly necessary cookies needed to run the site; and Customise, which lets you toggle analytics and marketing cookies individually before saving your preference. Whichever option you choose is remembered on your device for approximately six months, after which the banner will appear again so you can confirm or change your preference.

3. Cookies we use

We keep our cookie use deliberately small. The categories below cover everything currently in use on this site.

3.1 Strictly necessary

These run regardless of your banner choice because the site cannot function properly without them. On City News Bulletin this includes a first-party cookie (sd_cookie_consent) that records your cookie banner decision — accepted, rejected, or customised — and the categories you selected, so we don’t show the banner on every single page load.

3.2 Functional

If enabled, functional storage may remember small preferences related to how you browse the site, such as whether you’ve already dismissed a particular notice, to make repeat visits smoother.

3.3 Analytics

If you accept all cookies or enable analytics through Customise, we may use basic, privacy-conscious analytics to understand which pages are visited and roughly how the site is used, so we can improve navigation and content over time. This data is used in aggregate and is not used to build an individual profile of you for advertising purposes.

3.4 Advertising

If you accept all cookies or enable marketing through Customise, limited marketing cookies may be used to measure campaign effectiveness or deliver relevant content. We do not currently run extensive third-party advertising networks on this site. If that ever changes, this notice will be updated in advance and the choice will remain governed by the same banner described above.

4. Third-party cookies

Where we rely on third-party resources — for example, analytics or font providers — those providers may set their own cookies or collect connection data under their own privacy terms, separate from City News Bulletin’s. We choose providers with a reasonable privacy track record, but we encourage you to review their own policies if you have specific concerns.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Beyond our on-site banner, most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings menu. Doing so may affect how some parts of the site behave — for instance, the cookie banner may reappear more often if your browser is set to clear storage automatically between sessions. Because our own cookie footprint is small, blocking cookies entirely should not prevent you from reading any page or reaching us through our contact details.

6. How long choices last

Your cookie banner decision is stored for around six months from the date you make it. After that period, or if you clear your browser’s cookies or local storage manually, you’ll be asked again the next time you visit.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this Cookie Policy if the cookies we use change. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the latest version, and material changes will be reflected in the banner itself where appropriate.

8. Questions

For any question about cookies on City News Bulletin, contact us at privacy@citynewsbulletin.sg. See also our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data more broadly.
