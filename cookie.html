Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Singapore Daily uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences — such as cookie consent choices — over a period of time.

How we use cookies

When you first visit Singapore Daily, a banner invites you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your choices. Your selection is stored for six months, after which the banner may appear again so you can review your preferences.

Cookie categories

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. They include storing your cookie consent decision. Without them, some features may not work correctly.

  • sd_cookie_consent — stores your cookie banner choice. Duration: six months. Set by: Singapore Daily (first party).

Analytics cookies (optional)

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed most often. We enable analytics cookies only if you accept them through the banner or save customised preferences with analytics enabled.

Marketing cookies (optional)

Marketing cookies may be used to measure campaign effectiveness or deliver relevant content. These cookies are off by default until you opt in through the banner.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner — use “Reject all” to decline non-essential cookies, or “Customise” to toggle analytics and marketing individually.
  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Consult your browser help documentation for instructions.

Clearing cookies will reset your consent choice and may cause the cookie banner to reappear on your next visit.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change our use of cookies or adjust consent mechanics. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
