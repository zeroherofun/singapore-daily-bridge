Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Cookie Policy describes how City News Bulletin uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences over a period of time.

What we use

  • Preference storage: remembers whether you accepted, rejected, or customised cookie choices.
  • Essential operation: basic settings needed for navigation, display, and consent management.
  • Analytics (optional): helps us understand aggregate traffic patterns when you opt in.
  • Marketing (optional): may be used to measure campaign effectiveness when you opt in.

Cookie banner

When you first visit City News Bulletin, a banner invites you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your choices. Your selection is stored for six months, after which the banner may appear again so you can review your preferences.

Essential cookies

  • sd_cookie_consent — stores your cookie banner choice. Duration: six months. Set by: City News Bulletin (first party).

Managing cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner — use “Reject all” to decline non-essential cookies, or “Customise” to toggle analytics and marketing individually.
  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Consult your browser help documentation for instructions.

Clearing cookies will reset your consent choice and may cause the cookie banner to reappear on your next visit.

More information

For broader data practices, see our Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change our use of cookies or adjust consent mechanics. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
