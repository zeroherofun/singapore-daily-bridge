Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Singapore Daily uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how pages are used.

How we use cookies

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand visitor traffic and improve the site.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices such as cookie consent.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may delete existing cookies or block new ones. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.
