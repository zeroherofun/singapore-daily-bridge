Cookie policy

Introduction

This Cookie Policy ("Policy") describes how our website (the "Site") uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and personalise content and advertising for you. By using our Site, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to improve your user experience, store your preferences and help websites work more efficiently. Cookies can be session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (which remain on your device for a specific period of time).

Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies to improve the functionality and performance of our Site, personalise your experience and provide relevant content and advertising. Cookies also help us understand how you interact with our Site so that we can improve it in the future.

What types of cookies do we use?

We use several types of cookies on our Site, including:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly and without them some features may not work. They do not collect any personally identifiable information.
  • Performance Cookies: These cookies collect information about how you use our Site, such as which pages you visit most often. This information helps us improve our Site and your experience on it.
  • Functionality Cookies: These cookies allow us to remember your preferences, such as language settings, and provide personalised features.
  • Advertising cookies: These cookies track your browsing habits so that we can show you adverts that are more relevant to you. They may also be used by third party advertisers to target adverts to you.

How can you control cookies?

You can control and manage cookies in a number of ways:

  • You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or specify when a cookie is sent.
  • You can delete cookies that have already been set.
  • You can opt out of receiving targeted advertising from third-party advertisers.
