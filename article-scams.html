Police warn public about rise in phishing scams targeting bank customers

By Marcus Ng 5 min read

The Singapore Police Force said it has observed a sharp increase in phishing cases in which victims receive SMS messages or calls impersonating banks and government agencies.

In the latest advisory, officers urged members of the public not to click on links in unsolicited messages and to verify account alerts directly through official banking apps or websites.

Investigators said scammers often create urgency by claiming suspicious transactions or frozen accounts. Victims who provide one-time passwords or transfer funds rarely recover the full amount.

Anyone who suspect they have fallen prey to a scam is advised to call their bank immediately and lodge a report through the ScamShield helpline or the police online portal.
