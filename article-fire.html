SCDF responds to rubbish chute fire at Bukit Batok block; no injuries reported

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

The Singapore Civil Defence Force extinguished a rubbish chute fire at a Housing Board block in Bukit Batok on Tuesday evening. No injuries were reported and residents were not evacuated.

SCDF said it received a call at 7:42 pm reporting smoke on several upper floors of Block 273. Two fire engines and a red rhino were dispatched. Crews isolated the affected chute compartment and brought the blaze under control within 20 minutes.

Preliminary investigations suggest the fire was caused by discarded items that ignited inside the chute. Town council officers inspected the compartment and arranged for cleaning crews to clear residual debris overnight.

Residents on affected floors were advised to keep chute hoppers closed and avoid disposing of flammable materials. SCDF reminded households to wrap oily waste securely and not to throw lit cigarettes into rubbish chutes.
