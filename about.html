About Singapore Daily

Singapore Daily is a news aggregator focused on stories from Singapore and the wider region. We curate coverage across local affairs, business, technology, lifestyle, and international developments relevant to readers in the city-state.

Our editorial team selects headlines and summaries from trusted public sources, organising them by topic so visitors can quickly find updates on transport, housing, community events, and regional news. Singapore Daily does not publish original reporting; article pages present condensed summaries for general reference.

We aim to provide a straightforward reading experience with clear navigation, consistent formatting, and links to related stories. For corrections, feedback, or enquiries, please visit our Contact page.
