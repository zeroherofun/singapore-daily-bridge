New sheltered linkway opens between MRT station and bus interchange

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

A covered linkway connecting an MRT station to the adjacent bus interchange opened to the public on Monday, giving commuters a sheltered route between train and bus services. The 180-metre walkway includes barrier-free access and wider passages for peak-hour crowds.

The Land Transport Authority said the linkway was built to reduce exposure to rain and sun during transfers, particularly for elderly passengers and families with strollers. Directional signage and floor markings guide users from platform level to the bus boarding berths.

Commuters welcomed the new connection, noting that previously they had to cross an open plaza during wet weather. LTA said similar linkways are planned at two other interchange stations over the next year.
