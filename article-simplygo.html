LTA updates SimplyGo app with clearer fare display for commuters

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

The Land Transport Authority rolled out an update to the SimplyGo mobile app on Friday, showing card balances and trip fares immediately after commuters tap in and out at MRT gates and bus readers.

Previously, some users reported delays of up to several hours before journey charges appeared in the app. The new version displays the deducted amount on the home screen within seconds of each tap-out, along with a running daily total.

LTA said the change follows feedback collected during a public trial involving 15,000 SimplyGo account holders. The authority also added a trip history filter that groups journeys by date and payment card.

Commuters using physical EZ-Link or NETS FlashPay cards can continue to check balances at top-up machines and selected bus fare readers. LTA said further improvements, including push notifications for failed deductions, are planned for later this year.
