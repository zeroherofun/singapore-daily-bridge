New park connector linking Punggol to Coney Island opens to public

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

A 3.2 km park connector linking Punggol town to Coney Island was officially opened on Sunday, giving residents a dedicated cycling and walking route through coastal greenery and mangrove areas.

The National Parks Board said the path includes rest shelters, bicycle parking, and viewing decks overlooking the Serangoon Reservoir. Signage along the route highlights native bird species commonly spotted in the area.

The connector completes a missing segment in the North-Eastern Riverine Loop, allowing cyclists to travel from Punggol Waterway Park to Coney Island without using main roads. NParks advised visitors to keep to marked paths to protect sensitive habitats.

Weekend guided walks will be held for the first month to familiarise the public with the route. The board said it will monitor visitor numbers and may introduce one-way cycling lanes during peak periods if congestion occurs.
