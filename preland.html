Murder on Chin Swee Road: A late-night dispute between friends ended in a deadly attack

Updated
Published
Facebook Telegram More
Whatsapp Linkedin Twitter FB Messenger Email Print Copy permalinkCopy to clipboard

A late-night dispute between friends on Chin Swee Road ended in a deadly attack. A quarrel over SGD 137,000 escalated into a knife fight. One of the suspects later admitted it was an act of revenge for being cheated.

The shocking murder on Chin Swee Road has taken an unexpected turn. The suspect brought to the crime scene, 59-year-old Mr Poh, admitted that the killing was an act of revenge against his best friend for betrayal. He also pointed police to a key piece of evidence that later helped uncover the complicated case.

It was later revealed that the death of the 56-year-old man was the result of a fight and a long-running dispute between friends over a large sum of SGD 137,000.

«She went crazy over that money. She kept spending it, got herself lovers, and was sending them money. But we earned it together through investments in just 3 months! We have 94,500 dollars on Celestia Valexor!» the suspect claimed.

Investigators were initially confused by what the suspect was saying. However, they found a mobile phone belonging to his deceased wife. On it, they discovered the Celestia Valexor app, showing a balance of indeed 94,500 dollars.

According to the investigation team, the man and woman were unemployed and had traveled to Singapore from Indonesia. How could two unemployed Indonesian citizens have earned more than 94,000 Singapore dollars? Investigators were able to answer this question after conducting a detailed inquiry.

It turned out that the couple had first visited Singapore in June 2025. At a restaurant, they met a 45-year-old man. During a conversation about ways to make money, the man told them about an app called Celestia Valexor, which had allowed him to earn 2,800 dollars from an initial 350-dollar investment. This was confirmed by investigators after analyzing the suspect’s phone data.

According to the accused, the man showed them his phone, where the app was running. He explained that it worked using artificial intelligence that automatically invested funds into high-yield market assets.

After that, the accused and his wife decided to try it themselves. They exchanged their savings from Indonesian rupiah to Singapore dollars and registered on Celestia Valexor while in Singapore.

«In the first month, we earned 27,000 dollars. I couldn’t believe it and expected some kind of trick. But we withdrew the money to our bank card. The program really worked! But my wife and I started constantly fighting over this money,» said the murder suspect.

How did an ordinary family manage to earn 94,500 dollars in just 3 months? The ST editorial team conducted its own investigation, the results of which surprised many.

We found information in the Singapore Financial Organizations Registry: Celestia Valexor is an online investment platform developed with the support of financial regulators as part of the “Artificial Intelligence Implementation Strategy in Singapore.”

Experts from four leading Singaporean universities developed the «Auto-Trade» technology. This technology automatically analyzed data from 150,000 marketplaces and exchanges to identify potentially profitable trades.

Testing of the «Auto-Trade» technology produced impressive results: 83.7% of trades executed by the AI generated profits. These findings were verified by five independent commissions, resulting in the creation of the Celestia Valexor platform, available exclusively to Singaporean citizens.

Celestia Valexor was announced at the annual conference on artificial intelligence development in Singapore in May 2025. According to MAS statistics, more than 60,000 Singaporean citizens are already using Celestia Valexor, with their combined earnings exceeding 17,000,000 Singapore dollars.

«Celestia Valexor is not a magical platform. It’s a platform built on proven AI algorithms—a modern investment tool that provides stable passive income. Singaporean citizens can use it to earn income and help grow our nation’s economy. And most importantly, anyone can do it—students, working professionals, retirees. You don’t need to track numbers or charts. Just click a button, and the AI does the rest,» said Simon Teng, a representative of the Financial Supervisory Commission.

We were unable to find detailed public information about Celestia Valexor, but we did manage to speak with MAS Deputy Chairman Mr. Lim. He explained that registration for Celestia Valexor is open to all Singapore residents until 15.11.2025 (adaptive date +7 days), after which it will become limited.

Mr. Lim also explained how to start earning passive income on the platform:

Step 1: Registration. Go to the official Celestia Valexor website and fill out the registration form.

Step 2: Account verification. To verify your account, you must answer a call from a platform representative (important: make sure to provide your active phone number during registration).

Step 3: Account funding. Make your first deposit to your Celestia Valexor investment account using a bank card, electronic payment systems, or cryptocurrency. The minimum deposit is 350 SGD.

Step 4: AI system activation. Within 5 minutes after depositing funds, the “Auto-Trade” system will be activated. The system will start operating, analyzing the market.

Step 5: Trading. Launch automated trading at any convenient time, withdraw funds, or reinvest your earnings.

But how does Celestia Valexor really work? ST correspondent Benjamin Goh decided to find out.

He completed registration, deposited 350 dollars provided by the editorial team into his account, and began testing the platform.

«At times it felt like just a game. After confirming my phone and funding my account, the AI system began increasing the balance. It was buying and selling company stocks and cryptocurrency so fast that I couldn’t even keep up. I earned my first profit of 18 dollars within the first 5 minutes!

I tested the platform for 7 days. Each day I analyzed trade statistics and calculated profits. I found that roughly every 7 trades brought profit, while 1 resulted in a small loss. I calculated that, over time, each invested dollar could generate about 4–5 dollars in return.
