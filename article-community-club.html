Residents welcome new community club opening in Punggol West

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

A new community club in Punggol West opened its doors on Saturday, offering residents access to fitness studios, multi-purpose halls, and co-working spaces within walking distance of nearby HDB estates.

The three-storey facility replaces a temporary site that had served the area since 2019. It includes an indoor sports hall, rooms for eldercare programmes, and a youth zone with study areas and digital literacy workshops.

Grassroots advisers said the club was designed to support Punggol's growing population, particularly young families and seniors who previously had to travel to other towns for certain activities. Membership registration began online last week and more than 2,400 households have signed up.

Opening-day activities included health screenings, cooking demonstrations, and guided tours of the building's rooftop community garden. Regular operating hours begin Monday, with evening classes scheduled for working adults.
