Senior fitness classes expand to ten more community centres

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

Senior-friendly fitness classes will be offered at ten additional community centres from next month, building on a pilot programme that drew steady attendance from residents aged 60 and above. Sessions include low-impact aerobics, balance training, and guided stretching.

Certified instructors lead the classes on weekday mornings, with modified routines designed for participants with varying mobility levels. Registration is open to all seniors living in the surrounding precincts, and walk-in slots are available where space permits.

Community centre managers said early feedback highlighted the social aspect of the programme, with many participants staying on for coffee and conversation after class. Updated schedules and venue lists are posted on each centre's notice board and website.
