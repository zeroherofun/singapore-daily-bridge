Privacy Policy

How City News Bulletin collects, uses, and looks after personal data on this website.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

1. The short version

We collect personal data mainly when you email us through our contact page — typically a name, email address, and the substance of your message. We use it to reply to you, handle editorial corrections, and keep basic records of correspondence. We do not sell personal data to third parties. This notice explains the detail behind that summary, including what happens if you simply browse our site without contacting us, and how optional cookies work if you choose to accept them through our banner.

2. Who is responsible for your data

City News Bulletin, operating as a Singapore-focused news aggregation website, is the organisation responsible for personal data collected through this site, for the purposes of Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). You can reach us about any privacy matter at privacy@citynewsbulletin.sg or through our contact page.

3. Two contexts: reading the site and contacting us

We think it’s clearer to separate what we collect when you read news from what we collect when you write to us, because the two happen in different ways and for different reasons.

3.1 When you browse without contacting us

If you read City News Bulletin without submitting anything, our web server and hosting provider may log basic technical information automatically — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps — for security and performance purposes, in line with standard hosting practice. This is not linked to your identity unless you separately contact us.

Our website also uses a small number of cookies, described in full in our separate Cookie Policy. In short: essential cookies keep the site functioning, and any additional analytics or marketing cookies only run if you choose “Accept all” or enable them through “Customise” in our cookie banner. Choices made through the banner are stored on your device for approximately six months before you are asked again.

3.2 When you contact us

If you email us or use details on our contact page, we process the information you provide — your name, email address, and message content — so we can respond. We may also keep a record of that correspondence for editorial, complaint-handling, or operational purposes. We do not require registration or an account to read news on this site.

4. Why we use your data

We use personal data to: respond to enquiries, corrections, and feedback sent to our editorial team; operate and secure our website; understand aggregate traffic patterns where you have opted in to analytics cookies; and comply with legal or record-keeping obligations under Singapore law. We do not use contact details for unrelated marketing unless you separately and clearly opt in at a later date.

5. Our legal basis under the PDPA

Under the PDPA, we rely primarily on your consent when you contact us and expect a reply. Where relevant, we may also rely on the “legitimate interests” exception under the PDPA — for example to keep basic security logs of website traffic — or on legal obligation where records must be retained for a set period under applicable law.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share limited personal data with: our email or hosting providers, purely to deliver messages or operate the site; analytics providers, only where you have accepted optional analytics cookies; and authorities or advisers where required by law. Where any provider processes data outside Singapore, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data continues to receive a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. How long we keep data

Editorial enquiries and related correspondence are generally kept for up to twenty-four months from your last contact with us, after which they are deleted or anonymised unless a longer period is required for legal or dispute-resolution purposes. Cookie consent choices are held on your own device for approximately six months, as noted in our Cookie Policy, and are not stored on our servers as identifiable personal data.

8. Keeping data secure

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a news website — restricting access to correspondence to the people who need it to respond, using reputable third-party providers for hosting and email delivery, and avoiding unnecessary collection of sensitive personal data through our public pages. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we aim to handle your data with appropriate care.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can ask us to: confirm whether we hold personal data about you and provide access to it; correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data; and withdraw consent for us to continue collecting, using, or disclosing your personal data, subject to legal or contractual restrictions that may apply. To make any of these requests, email privacy@citynewsbulletin.sg with enough detail for us to locate your record — typically the name and email address you used, and roughly when you contacted us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required under the PDPA.

10. Children’s data

This website is intended for general readership. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through our public pages, and any contact to our editorial team is expected to be made by an adult on their own behalf.

11. Marketing and the Do Not Call Registry

We do not send unsolicited marketing messages based on a routine editorial enquiry. If we ever introduce an opt-in mailing list for news briefings, joining it will require a clear, separate action on your part, and every message will include a straightforward way to unsubscribe. Where any marketing call or SMS to a Singapore telephone number is contemplated, we will check the number against Singapore’s Do Not Call Registry beforehand, as required under the PDPA, unless you have given clear consent that overrides that check.

12. Automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Enquiries sent to our editorial team are read and actioned by a person, not by an automated system.

13. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The “last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Where a change is significant — for example, a new category of data collected or a new third party we share data with — we will aim to flag it clearly on this page. We encourage you to check back occasionally, particularly before contacting us with a new enquiry.

14. Contact us about privacy

For any question about this notice, or to exercise your rights under the PDPA, contact City News Bulletin at privacy@citynewsbulletin.sg or see our contact page.
