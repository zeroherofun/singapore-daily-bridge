StarHub says broadband outage in eastern Singapore fully resolved

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

StarHub said it has fully restored broadband services to customers in eastern Singapore after a fibre routing fault disrupted connections for several hours on Monday afternoon.

The telco first detected abnormal traffic patterns at a node serving Bedok, Tampines, and parts of Marine Parade shortly after 2 pm. Engineers rerouted affected circuits and deployed field teams to inspect underground links near an construction zone.

By 6:30 pm, StarHub reported that more than 98 per cent of impacted lines were back online. Remaining cases involved individual home setups that required on-site modem resets, the company said in an update posted on its service status page.

StarHub apologised to affected customers and said it will issue service credits where downtime exceeded its published thresholds. The Infocomm Media Development Authority said it is reviewing the incident with the operator.
