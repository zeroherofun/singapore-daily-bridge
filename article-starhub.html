Nationwide drill tests emergency broadcast alerts on mobile networks

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

A nationwide drill tested Singapore's emergency broadcast alert system on mobile networks on Wednesday morning, sending sample messages to compatible phones across the island. The exercise was clearly marked as a test and required no action from recipients.

The drill checked delivery times and message display across different handset models and network operators. Officials said the system is designed to reach users quickly during urgent situations such as severe weather warnings or public safety notices.

Members of the public who did not receive the test message were advised to ensure their phone software is up to date and that emergency alerts are enabled in device settings. Results from the drill will be published in a summary report next month.
