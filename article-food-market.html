Weekend food market returns to Marina Bay with local hawker favourites

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

A weekend food market featuring stalls from established hawker centres reopened at Marina Bay on Friday, drawing office workers and tourists to a row of temporary kitchens set up along the waterfront promenade.

Twenty vendors from Maxwell, Old Airport Road, and Tiong Bahru are taking part in the six-week run, serving dishes such as Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, and rojak. Organisers said the market aims to give smaller stallholders exposure beyond their usual neighbourhoods.

Seating areas with shade structures have been arranged near The Promontory, and additional waste collection points were added to manage lunchtime crowds. The market operates from 11 am to 9 pm on Fridays through Sundays.

Visitors can pay by cash or contactless methods at individual stalls. The National Environment Agency said it will conduct regular inspections to ensure food hygiene standards are maintained throughout the event period.
