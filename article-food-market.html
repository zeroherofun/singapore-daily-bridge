Heritage walking trail launches in Kampong Glam district

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

A new heritage walking trail has opened in the Kampong Glam district, guiding visitors past historic shophouses, mosques, and cultural landmarks that shaped the neighbourhood over more than a century. Colourful mural markers along the route highlight key stops and suggested photo points.

The self-guided trail covers roughly two kilometres and can be completed in about 90 minutes at a leisurely pace. Printed maps are available at the Malay Heritage Centre, and a mobile-friendly version includes short audio clips describing each location.

Guided tours led by local volunteers will run on selected Saturday mornings. Organisers said the trail complements existing dining and retail offerings in the area, encouraging visitors to explore side alleys they might otherwise overlook.
