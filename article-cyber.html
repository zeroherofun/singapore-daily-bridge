Public urged to verify official SMS links after reports of spoofed healthcare messages

Healthcare agencies and the Cyber Security Agency have reminded residents to check the sender details and web addresses of SMS messages before opening links related to appointments or test results.

Officials said several reports involved messages that closely mimicked government formatting, including shortened URLs that redirected users to credential-harvesting pages.

Patients are advised to use verified portals and mobile apps to manage appointments. Clinics have been told to avoid including clickable links in routine reminders unless they use approved domains.

The advisory forms part of a broader public education campaign on digital hygiene ahead of the year-end travel season.
