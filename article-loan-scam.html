Man jailed 14 months for cheating victims in loan scam case

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

A 34-year-old man was sentenced to 14 months' jail on Wednesday after pleading guilty to three counts of cheating victims who responded to online loan advertisements.

Between March and June last year, the man posed as a licensed moneylender and collected upfront "processing fees" ranging from SGD 200 to SGD 800 from at least six victims. None of the promised loans were ever disbursed.

Deputy Public Prosecutor Lim Wei Ming told the court that the accused used messaging apps to contact victims and directed payments to personal bank accounts. Police traced the transactions after several victims filed reports with the Anti-Scam Centre.

Distressing the court, the man had been previously cautioned for similar conduct. In sentencing, the judge noted that loan scams continue to target vulnerable borrowers and ordered restitution hearings to be scheduled for affected victims.
