LTA steps up enforcement against speeding and reckless riding in school zones

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

The Land Transport Authority will deploy additional enforcement officers and mobile cameras near primary schools during morning and afternoon peak hours for the rest of the term.

The move follows a rise in complaints about motorists exceeding the 40 km/h limit and personal mobility device riders weaving through pedestrian crossings outside several schools in the east and north-east.

LTA said it recorded 312 offences in school zones last month, including speeding, failure to give way to pedestrians, and illegal PMD use on footpaths adjacent to school gates.

Parents' groups welcomed the initiative but urged schools to stagger dismissal times where possible. The authority said it will review traffic patterns at 28 priority locations and publish a summary of findings before the next school term.
