The Situation on Chin Swee Road: How the Late-Night Argument Between Friends Ended

By David Tan 6 min read

What began as a late-night disagreement has developed into a case that continues to raise questions. New information has emerged about the people involved and the events that unfolded after midnight.

Police from the Central Police Division were alerted to Chin Swee Road in the early hours after residents reported a disturbance near a shophouse corridor. Officers secured the area and began speaking to witnesses who said raised voices had been heard before the situation escalated.

According to preliminary accounts, two men who had known each other for many years were involved in the dispute. Neighbours described their relationship as generally cordial until tensions grew over the past year. Investigators are reviewing statements and any available footage to establish a clearer timeline.

A spokesperson said enquiries remain active and that updates would be shared when appropriate. Several residents submitted doorbell camera clips to assist officers, while community leaders urged calm and asked anyone with information to come forward through official channels.

Counselling support has been offered to families in the block nearest the incident. Traffic along Chin Swee Road returned to normal by morning, though a visible police presence remained through the day as follow-up work continued.

City News Bulletin will update this report as authorities release further details. Readers with direct information are advised to contact the police rather than share unverified claims online.
