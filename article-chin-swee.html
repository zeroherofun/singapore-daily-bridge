Murder on Chin Swee Road: A late-night dispute between friends ended in a deadly attack

By David Tan 8 min read

A late-night dispute between friends on Chin Swee Road ended in a deadly attack. A quarrel over SGD 137,000 escalated into a knife fight. One of the suspects later admitted it was an act of revenge for being cheated.

The shocking murder on Chin Swee Road has taken an unexpected turn. The suspect brought to the crime scene, 59-year-old Mr Poh, admitted that the killing was an act of revenge against his best friend for betrayal. He also pointed police to a key piece of evidence that later helped uncover the complicated case.

It was later revealed that the death of the 56-year-old man was the result of a fight and a long-running dispute between friends over a large sum of SGD 137,000.

Officers from the Central Police Division were alerted to the incident shortly after midnight. Paramedics pronounced the victim dead at the scene. Witnesses said an argument could be heard from a nearby shophouse corridor before the attack took place.

According to preliminary statements, the two men had known each other for more than two decades and had jointly managed several business ventures. Tensions mounted when one party accused the other of withholding proceeds from a recent transaction worth SGD 137,000.

Mr Poh was arrested at the scene and taken for medical assessment before being questioned. Police said he appeared cooperative and provided details that led officers to a storage unit containing financial records relevant to the dispute.

Neighbours told reporters they heard raised voices before the altercation but did not see the attack itself. Several residents submitted doorbell camera footage to assist with the timeline of events.

Investigators are reviewing bank transfer records and messages exchanged between the two men over the past six months. A spokesperson said the dispute appeared to have intensified after one party claimed the other had misused shared funds.

Community leaders urged residents to remain calm and to contact the police if they have any information. Counselling support has been made available to families living in the block nearest to the incident.

The case has been classified as murder. If convicted, the suspect faces life imprisonment or the death penalty. Investigations are ongoing, and police have urged anyone with information to contact the hotline or submit tips through the official online portal.

This report will be updated as more details are confirmed by the authorities.
