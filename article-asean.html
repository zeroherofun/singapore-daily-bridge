Regional leaders meet in Jakarta to discuss trade and climate initiatives

By Priya Nair 5 min read

Leaders from Southeast Asian nations gathered in Jakarta for a working summit focused on supply chain resilience, cross-border energy projects and coordinated responses to extreme weather events.

Delegates discussed simplifying customs procedures for green technology components and expanding early-warning systems for floods and heatwaves across the region.

Singapore's representative highlighted ongoing cooperation on digital trade standards and port connectivity, noting that smaller economies would benefit from shared technical assistance programmes.
