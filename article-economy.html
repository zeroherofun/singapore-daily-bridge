Singapore GDP growth beats forecasts as services sector expands

Singapore's economy expanded more strongly than expected in the third quarter, with gross domestic product rising 3.2% compared with the same period last year, according to advance estimates from the Ministry of Trade and Industry.

The services sector led the growth, particularly finance and insurance, wholesale trade, and accommodation and food services. Manufacturing activity also picked up, supported by electronics exports to key markets in Asia and the United States.

The Monetary Authority of Singapore said it would maintain its current policy stance, noting that core inflation has moderated but remains above the long-term average.
