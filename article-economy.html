Retail Updated

Local retailers report steady footfall during mid-year sale period

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

Shop owners in Orchard Road, Bugis, and Jurong reported steady footfall during the mid-year sale period, with moderate crowds on weekends and consistent weekday traffic from office workers and nearby residents. Several malls extended opening hours to accommodate evening shoppers.

Tenant associations said apparel and lifestyle stores saw the strongest visitor numbers, while dining outlets benefited from families combining shopping trips with meals. Promotional displays and in-mall events helped draw passers-by into smaller independent shops along side streets.

Retailers described the atmosphere as calm rather than crowded, with manageable queues at popular outlets. Many plan to maintain selected discounts for another fortnight before returning to regular pricing.
