Relief Updated

Singapore sends relief supplies after flooding in neighbouring region

By Staff Reporter 4 min read

Singapore has dispatched a shipment of relief supplies to a neighbouring region affected by recent flooding, including bottled water, hygiene kits, and portable shelter materials. The consignment left Changi Airfreight Centre on Tuesday under coordination with local humanitarian partners.

Officials said the supplies will be distributed through established community networks in the worst-hit districts. Singapore agencies are working with regional counterparts to identify additional needs as floodwaters recede and access to remote areas improves.

Volunteer groups in Singapore have also collected donated items such as blankets and children's clothing for a second shipment planned next week. Updates on the relief effort will be shared through the foreign affairs ministry's public information channels.
