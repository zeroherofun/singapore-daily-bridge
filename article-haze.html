NEA warns of haze from regional hotspots; PSI may enter unhealthy range

By Marcus Ng 4 min read

The National Environment Agency said prevailing winds could bring smoke haze from increasing hotspot activity in parts of Sumatra and Kalimantan over the next few days.

The 24-hour Pollutant Standards Index may enter the unhealthy range if wind conditions persist. Vulnerable groups, including the elderly and children, are advised to reduce prolonged outdoor activity.

Agencies are monitoring air quality hourly and will issue updates through the NEA website and the myENV mobile app.
