Thundery showers expected over southern Singapore this week

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

The National Environment Agency forecasts thundery showers over southern and eastern parts of Singapore on most afternoons this week, driven by light wind conditions and daytime heating. Short spells of moderate to heavy rain are expected between 2 pm and 6 pm on several days.

NEA said the showers are likely to be localised and may cause temporary ponding in low-lying areas. Temperatures are expected to range between 25 and 33 degrees Celsius, with slightly cooler conditions during rain episodes.

Members of the public are advised to carry an umbrella when heading out and to take shelter if thunder is heard. Updated forecasts are available on the NEA website and weather app throughout the week.
