Murder on Chin Swee Road: A late-night dispute between friends ended in a deadly attack

A quarrel over SGD 137,000 escalated into a knife fight. One of the suspects later admitted it was an act of revenge for being cheated.

The shocking murder on Chin Swee Road has taken an unexpected turn. The suspect brought to the crime scene, 59-year-old Mr Poh, admitted that the killing was an act of revenge against his best friend for betrayal.

It was later revealed that the death of the 56-year-old man was the result of a fight and a long-running dispute between friends over a large sum of SGD 137,000.
