Murder on Chin Swee Road: A late-night dispute between friends ended in a deadly attack

A heated argument between long-time friends escalated into a knife fight shortly after midnight. One of the suspects later admitted the attack was an act of revenge following a bitter falling-out.

The murder on Chin Swee Road has taken an unexpected turn. The suspect brought to the crime scene, 59-year-old Mr Poh, admitted that the killing was an act of revenge against his friend. He also pointed police to a key piece of evidence.

Police said the death of the 56-year-old man was the result of a fight and a long-running personal dispute. Officers from the Central Police Division were alerted shortly after midnight.
