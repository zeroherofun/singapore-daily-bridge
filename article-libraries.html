National Gallery launches free weekday tours for student groups

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

The National Gallery Singapore has launched a programme of free guided tours for school groups on weekday mornings. The tours cover selected Southeast Asian and Singapore collections and are led by trained docents who adapt their walk-throughs to different age groups.

Schools must register at least two weeks in advance through an online booking portal. Each session accommodates up to 30 students and includes a short introduction to gallery etiquette and the history of the City Hall and former Supreme Court buildings.

The gallery said the initiative aims to make art more accessible to local students during term time. Additional slots will be added during the mid-year break, and teachers can download preparatory materials from the gallery website before their visit.
