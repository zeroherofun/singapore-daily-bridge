Libraries extend opening hours during school holiday period

By Staff Reporter 3 min read

Public libraries across Singapore will stay open until 9 pm on weekdays and 8 pm on Sundays throughout the school holiday period, the National Library Board announced on Monday.

The extended hours apply to 18 branch libraries in housing estates with high student footfall, including Jurong Regional Library, Tampines Regional Library, and library@harbourfront. Regular opening times resume once schools reopen.

NLB said additional study seats and quiet zones will be available at selected branches. Holiday programmes including storytelling sessions, coding workshops for children, and author talks will run on a revised schedule posted on the board's website.

Members can continue to borrow and return items at self-service kiosks outside standard hours at libraries equipped with book drops. NLB encouraged visitors to check branch-specific timings before heading down, as refurbishment works at two smaller outlets may affect access on certain days.
